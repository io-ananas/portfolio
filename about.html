Hello, I'm Ioana.

I'm a designer based in Copenhagen, with over 10 years of experience shaping visual identities and digital experiences for brands that care about clarity, craft, and meaning.

Originally from France, with Greek heritage, I've worked across different countries and international environments. This has shaped my perspective, strengthened my adaptability, and influenced a more thoughtful, user-centered approach to design.

Over time, my work has naturally evolved toward creating experiences that are not only visually engaging, but also intuitive, accessible, and easy to use. I'm particularly interested in how design can reduce complexity and better support people in their everyday interactions with digital products.

I enjoy collaborating with people from diverse backgrounds and translating ideas into clear, meaningful design solutions.

Outside of work, you'll often find me reading fantasy books, hiking, wandering in nature, or exploring hands-on crafts.
