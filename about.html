Hello, I'm Ioana.

I'm a senior graphic designer based in Copenhagen with over 10 years of experience shaping visual identities for brands that care about craft and meaning. I believe design should feel inevitable — simple on the surface, considered underneath.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring analogue photography, rearranging my bookshelf, or cycling along the harbour.
